    Reading lists

    Good material for after the reveal.

    A first shelf for the conversations this site is meant to start: founding-era constitutional argument, Reformed political theology, magistracy, resistance, and the two kingdoms.

    Federalist and Anti-Federalist

    American founding arguments

    • The Federalist Papers, full textPrimary source collection for the Federalist side of ratification.
    • Introduction to the AntifederalistsHelpful orientation to the Anti-Federalist critique and the ratification debate.
    • The Constitution of the United StatesThe full constitutional text for the structure of the American republic.
    • The Bill of RightsThe first ten amendments, focused on written limits and protected liberties.
    • Democracy in America by Alexis de TocquevilleA classic outside observer on American democracy, habits, religion, local life, and liberty.
    Reformed resistance

    Rulers, subjects, and tyranny

    • Lex, Rex by Samuel RutherfordA classic Reformed argument that law stands over kingly power.
    • Concerning the Rights of Rulers by Theodore BezaResistance theory, obedience, tyranny, and the duties of rulers and subjects.
    • Historic political theology sourceA longer academic source worth reviewing alongside the Reformed resistance tradition.
    Magistracy

    Public duty before God

    • Pastoral and Political Discourses of Lemuel HaynesEarly American sermons and political reflection from Lemuel Haynes.
    • What Does the Bible Teach About the Responsibility of Those Who Govern the Nations?A contemporary Reformed treatment of civil rulers and national responsibility.
    Two kingdoms

    Church, state, and citizenship

    • Defining the Two KingdomsMichael Horton on Reformed two kingdoms categories and Christian citizenship.
    Western inheritances

    Greece, scholasticism, and reform

    • The Republic by PlatoA major source for questions about justice, the soul, education, rulers, and the good society.
    • Summa Theologiae by Thomas AquinasA central scholastic work for natural law, virtue, reason, theology, and the Aristotelian stream of the West.
